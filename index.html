How Quantsiv Works

From code scanning to actionable insights in 90 seconds

1. Discover

Quantsiv automatically scans your entire infrastructure - including source code, certificates, cloud resources, and configuration files - to build a complete inventory of your cryptographic assets. Our agentless approach means no installation required.

2. Analyze

Our quantum-risk engine analyzes each cryptographic finding against known quantum-vulnerable algorithms (RSA, ECDSA, Diffie-Hellman) using Shor's algorithm projections. We calculate exposure based on data sensitivity, public accessibility, and algorithm key strength.

3. Prioritize

Results are automatically ranked by risk level using our proprietary scoring system, giving you a clear, actionable remediation roadmap instead of an overwhelming list of vulnerabilities. Critical findings are highlighted for immediate attention.

4. Monitor

Continuous monitoring alerts you to new quantum-vulnerable code or configurations as they're introduced in your codebase, preventing regression while you address existing findings. Get real-time alerts via email, Slack, or dashboard notifications.

The entire process completes in under 90 seconds for most repositories, giving you immediate visibility into your quantum security posture.
