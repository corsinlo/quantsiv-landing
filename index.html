How it works

1. Discover

Quantsiv automatically scans your entire infrastructure - code, certificates, cloud resources, and configurations - to build a complete inventory of your cryptographic assets.

2. Analyze

Our quantum-risk engine analyzes each finding against known quantum-vulnerable algorithms (RSA, ECDSA, Diffie-Hellman) and calculates exposure based on data sensitivity and public accessibility.

3. Prioritize

Results are ranked by risk level, giving you a clear, actionable remediation roadmap instead of an overwhelming list of vulnerabilities.

4. Monitor

Continuous monitoring alerts you to new quantum-vulnerable code or configurations as they're introduced, preventing regression while you address existing findings.
